AMBEd for Windows x64 - Third-Party Notices

FTDI D2XX
==========

AMBEd communicates with supported FTDI-based USB vocoder devices through
Future Technology Devices International Limited (FTDI) D2XX.

The FTDI D2XX driver/runtime is proprietary third-party software and is NOT
included in this AMBEd Windows release package.

Obtain the Windows D2XX driver/runtime directly from FTDI:

https://ftdichip.com/drivers/d2xx-drivers/

FTDI states on its D2XX driver page that its drivers are for use with
products based on FTDI parts and that the drivers may be distributed as
long as the license information is not modified.

This package intentionally leaves the FTDI driver/runtime separate and
directs users to FTDI's official distribution.

FTDI and D2XX are trademarks or product names of their respective owner.
